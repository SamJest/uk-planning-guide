# Proposed change plan and preflight gate

Priority follows the controlling September brief. This is a staged recovery, not a redesign or corpus refresh.

## Preflight exit

The six named audits, raw Git/pack/HTML evidence and clean isolated build result must be present before implementation. Verify production identity, preserve existing changes, record observed baseline failures. A failing old build is a finding to repair, not permission to claim tests pass. No production deploy is allowed by audit completion alone.

## Phase0: publication containment

Retain current contracts and approved=false sign-off. Complete safe default scope: unknown modes reject, no standalone full output default, no unrestricted publish predicate. Disable stale full-corpus package entry points with clear replacement instructions. Build to a fresh isolated destination; report source fingerprint to invalidate stale checkpoints. Add negative tests for bypasses and unsafe output targets. Retain untracked34–37 untouched, document their retirement. Introduce a reviewed exact-file release manifest tied to production commit, before/after hashes, count delta and rollback map. Source commits on a new codex/recovery branch; never merge source history wholesale into main.

## Phase1: inventory and indexation

Ledger union of generated paths, submitted URLs, source records, redirects, protected traffic and historical examples. Unknowns explicit. Protect all1,000 click-bearing URLs,28 priority paths and fences/walls family; no path changes without evidence. Obtain current GSC examples and investigate single5xx first when known. Technical sitemap defects can be removed without deleting pages or making new canonical decisions. Family partitioning should preserve eligible membership, with separate editorial review queue—not blanket quality approval. Ship only tested cohorts with an explained sitemap count delta. Do not mass-remove unknown quality URLs or copy old404 groups into current buckets.

## Phase2: semantic recovery

Keep national/rule/project/authority concepts separate. Reject generic pages that receive inferred projects and unsupported multi-authority HMO combinations. Explicit schema, evidence sources and provenance required. Test required NorthYork PD, Glasgow conservation, Sheffield council, dropped-kerb and solar hubs, HMO/Article4, Durham fences, England/Scotland/Wales fixtures. Renew official-source checks before substantive planning claims. Small same-URL cohort, no corpus regeneration. Preserve July corrected pages unless independently defective.

## Phase3: journey

Retain HomeProof, existing route paths and browser-storage contracts. Consolidate presentation into Planning Route Check, Local Constraint Check, Project Readiness and My Planning Project after semantic fixes. Trace data flow before touching saved projects. No speculative paid funnel or replacement brand.

## Phase4: passive revenue

Source/quality readiness first. Implement default-off feature flags, eligibility and exclusions; no early third-party scripts. Consent/measurement with minimal enumerated events, no free text/address/project details in analytics. Named owner-approved expiring referral registry; no invented partners. Free readiness summary/demand test before price or paid launch. Payment/email providers, price/refunds, legal identity and partner approvals are owner decisions; safe code scaffolding does not imply live commercial activation. Hosted checkout, authenticated idempotent webhook and private delivery must be tested before charging anyone.

## Deferred / rejected

Reject unrestricted full sitemap restore, mass content regeneration, blanket redirects, fictional editorial desk, mechanically refreshed source dates and unverified legal conclusions. Defer commercial activation/account choices and GSC bucket-specific removals until evidence/access exists. No old source/pages reverted wholesale. Every completed phase updates reconciliation and a delivery report with files, commits, tests, counts, deployment and decisions outstanding.
